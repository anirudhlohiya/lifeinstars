Privacy policy

What we collect

Why

Only to make your video, deliver it to your email, send you a receipt, and help if something goes wrong. If you gave permission, we may also post your video on our Instagram page.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Your story and video are deleted from our servers 60 days after delivery. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires.
