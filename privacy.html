Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

What we collect

  • The story you enter: names, relations, ages and what happened.
  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Payment status from Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

Why

Only to make your video, deliver it to your email, send you a receipt, and help if something goes wrong. If you gave permission, we may also post your video on our Instagram page.

Who else sees it

  • Razorpay processes the payment (their own privacy policy applies).
  • Google: Gmail and Google Drive deliver the video, and a private Google Sheet keeps a backup copy of each order so nothing is lost if something goes wrong on our side.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

While you're filling the form

What you type is saved only in your own browser, so a refresh or a closed tab doesn't lose your work. It is never sent anywhere until you click "Pay & place order", and it's cleared from your browser once your payment goes through. You can also clear it any time with "Start over".

How long we keep it

Your story and video are deleted from our servers, Google Drive and the backup sheet 60 days after delivery. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires.

Your choices

Email gyaansharma22@gmail.com to see, correct or delete your data, or to remove a video we've posted.
