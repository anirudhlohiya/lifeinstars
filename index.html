Questions

Before you order

How long does it take?

Basic and Family plans are delivered within 3 days, Rush within 12 hours. Most videos are ready much sooner. You'll get an email the moment your order is received, and another when the video is ready.

What if I spot a mistake after paying?

Reply to the order email with the correction. If the video has already been sent, we'll remake it once for free within 7 days.

What if it's late, or I change my mind?

If your video arrives later than promised, you get a full refund. You can also cancel for a full refund any time before it's delivered. See the refund policy.

Is my family's information private?

Your story is used only to make your video. It's deleted 60 days after delivery, and we never post your video unless you tick the box allowing it. Payments are handled by Razorpay; we never see your card or UPI details. See the privacy policy.

Can I make one for someone who has passed away?

Yes. Choose "Someone I love", then "No, they have passed away". Their video ends with them becoming the brightest star among everyone they loved.

Who do I talk to if something goes wrong?

Email gyaansharma22@gmail.com with your order ID. A person replies within 24 hours.
